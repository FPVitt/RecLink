Troubleshooting

Start with the LED and the Status tab. They tell you which of the two links, flight controller or camera, is the problem.

Wi-Fi and the configurator

ProblemTry
I cannot see the RECLINK networkThe portal only runs while disarmed and with the flight controller link up. Check the aircraft is disarmed and the LED shows two quick blinks per second. Hold the board's button for 3 seconds to open it by hand. It also closes after 15 minutes idle, so hold again if it went away.
I joined, but the page will not loadType http://10.0.0.1 in the address bar. Stay connected even if the phone warns about no internet. Turn off mobile data if the phone keeps switching away. Up to three devices can connect at once.
The password is rejectedThe factory password is reclinkfpv. If you changed it and lost it, hold the button for 10 seconds to factory reset. That also erases camera pairings.
My changes disappearedPress Save settings. Changes apply and persist only after saving. The bar says "unsaved changes" until you do.

Flight controller and OSD

ProblemTry
Status shows FC offlineCheck wiring: FC TX to RecLink RX and FC RX to RecLink TX. Check MSP is enabled on that UART at 115200. Try Swap TX/RX pins (advanced), save and reboot. If you changed the baud, match it on both sides.
OSD shows nothing from RecLinkConfirm Betaflight is 2025.12 or newer, and that Custom message 1–4 are enabled and placed in the OSD tab. An OSD_UNSUPPORTED chip means the API is too old.
Placeholder names instead of textThe line is empty or RecLink is not connected yet. Check the FC link first.
OSD symbols look wrongMatch OSD type to your goggles. Digital filters to plain letters and digits. Use analog only for an analog OSD chip.
A line is cut offLines are limited to 16 characters. Shorten the template. The preview warns you.
The board resets in flightCheck the 5 V supply. Add a 220 µF or larger capacitor across the board's power pads. Watch for WDT_RECOVERED or a rising reboot counter in Diagnostics.

Camera

ProblemTry
The camera is not found when pairingGoPro: put it in Connections → Connect Device → The Remote first, then press Scan now. Disconnect it from any phone app. Move RecLink closer. Make sure fewer than four cameras are already paired.
"Bond list full"Four cameras are the limit. Forget one on the Binding tab.
REPAIR_NEEDED chip, or the camera never reconnectsThe camera forgot RecLink, usually after its connections were reset. Forget it on the Binding tab, then pair it again.
It connects, then drops again and againMove the board away from carbon and battery leads. In Network (advanced) raise Bluetooth power to Max and reboot. After three silent hangs in two minutes RecLink reboots itself to clear the link.
CAM LOST on the OSDThe camera is off, asleep, out of range or has dropped the link. Turn it on and wait; RecLink retries on its own.
Values on Status are blankThe camera has not confirmed them yet. Press Refresh camera state. Blank means unknown, on purpose.

Recording and zones

ProblemTry
It does not record on armCheck Start / stop recording is set to a mode that includes arm, and the Status tab shows the aircraft as armed. Check the camera has a card and is connected (READY).
REC FAIL on the OSDThe camera did not confirm recording. Check the SD card and that the camera is not busy. In advanced mode, raise Wait for camera to confirm or Retries. Open the Record trigger debug panel to see the last command.
The switch does nothingUse Set AUX again. Check the amber cursor on the bar moves when you flip it, and that the threshold sits between the off and on positions. For a push-button choose Trigger type: Button.
Zones do not change the cameraCheck Enable zone control, the right AUX channel, and that the camera is in a video preset and not recording. Wait for the hold time (400 ms). Read the Zone settings row on Status.
"partly applied (not accepted: …)"The camera refused those settings. Some options are not valid together, for example a frame rate with the chosen resolution. Pick values the lists offer, and use Capture from camera to start from a known-good combination.
Footage has the wrong dateClock sync needs a GPS fix with enough satellites and a camera that is idle and connected. See the Clock tab and press Sync now.
Still stuck

With Advanced mode on, the System tab shows a short log and the Status tab shows counters and three debug panels. A screenshot of those, plus your firmware version from the header, is what you need when asking for help.

Status LED

[[svg:led]]

Timings are nominal. On the production board the LED is a single-colour LED, so patterns differ by rhythm only. Boards with an addressable LED use colour too (blue for the portal, green for recording). The LED shows the highest-priority pattern that applies: portal, then a recent watchdog recovery, then recording, then flight controller down, then the camera link state. Identify overrides everything while it runs.

What RecLink can and cannot touch

It reads

  • Flight controller: API version, craft name, arm state, RC channels, GPS, battery state, clock.
  • Camera: recording, battery, SD, mode, resolution, frame rate and the other values shown on the Status tab.

It writes

  • Four lines of OSD text to the flight controller.
  • Record, settings, highlight and clock commands to the camera.

It never sends

  • Any RC, GPS, clock, display-port or reboot command to the flight controller.
  • Any command that arms or disarms.
  • Any formatting, firmware, reset or file-delete command to the camera from automation. Those exist only as labelled portal actions, while disarmed.

Glossary

AUXA spare radio channel beyond the four sticks. AUX 1 is channel 5.
µsMicroseconds. Channel values run from about 1000 (low) to 2000 (high).
BLEBluetooth Low Energy, the link to the camera.
BondThe stored pairing between RecLink and one camera.
MSPMultiWii Serial Protocol, the language Betaflight speaks over the wire.
OSDOn-screen display: the text drawn over your video feed.
OTAOver-the-air firmware update, done from the configurator.
PortalRecLink's Wi-Fi network and configuration web page.
ZoneA slice of a radio switch's travel that selects a set of camera settings.
