Quick start

RecLink

RecLink links your flight controller to your action camera. The camera records when you arm, and its status appears on your OSD. This page takes you from the box to your first recording.

Pre-release firmware

Firmware 0.1.0 has been bench-tested only and is not flight tested. Do every step below with the propellers removed, and check that everything works before you fly.

You need

  • The RecLink board
  • A flight controller with Betaflight 2025.12 or newer and a free UART
  • A GoPro (HERO 11 or newer) or a DJI Osmo (Action 4, 5, 6 or 360), charged, with an SD card
  • A phone or laptop

Time needed

About 15 minutes, once. After that, RecLink starts by itself every time you power the quad.

Step 1

Wire it

  • Connect the four wires as drawn. TX goes to RX, and RX goes to TX.
  • If the board resets by itself, add a 220 µF capacitor across its 5 V pads.
  • Mount it near the camera, away from carbon fibre.
  • Wires crossed by mistake? Fix it later in the configurator (Network → Swap TX/RX pins). No soldering.

Step 2

Set up Betaflight

Use the latest Betaflight Configurator

You need Betaflight firmware 2025.12.0 or newer on the flight controller, and the latest Betaflight Configurator on your computer. The screenshots in this guide were taken with Configurator 2026.6.2. Use the web app at app.betaflight.com (in Chrome or Edge) or install the newest desktop version. An older Configurator may not offer the Custom message elements.

[[shot name="bf_ports" cap="Betaflight Configurator → Ports." legend="1|Switch MSP on for the UART you wired.;2|Leave the speed at 115200."]]
  1. Check your Betaflight is 2025.12.0 or newer. Older versions cannot show RecLink's text. Then open Betaflight Configurator and go to the Ports tab. On the UART you wired, switch MSP on. Leave the speed at 115200. Save.

Then press Save and Reboot at the bottom right.

[[shot name="bf_osd" cap="OSD tab → Elements: Custom message 1 to 4 ticked." legend="1|Tick Custom message 1, 2, 3 and 4."]]
  1. Go to the OSD tab. Tick Custom message 1, 2, 3 and 4 and drag them to a free spot on the screen. Save.

Drag the four messages in the preview to a free spot, one under the other. Do not forget to Save.

[[shot name="bf_osd_preview" cap="OSD tab: the four Custom messages in the preview." w="78mm"]]
[[shot name="bf_receiver" cap="Receiver tab: the bar of your switch moves when you flip it." legend="1|AUX 2 moves to 2000 when the switch is flipped."]]
  1. On your radio, pick a spare switch if you want to start and stop recording by hand. In Betaflight's Receiver tab, flip it and check that an AUX bar moves. This is optional.

Step 3

Open the configurator

[[shot name="first_screen" cap="The configurator on your phone."]]
  1. Power the quad (propellers off). Wait a few seconds. The RecLink LED blinks twice every second when its Wi-Fi is on.
  2. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network RECLINK-XXXX. Password: reclinkfpv.
  3. Open http://10.0.0.1 in your browser. If the phone says there is no internet, stay connected.
No Wi-Fi network?

The Wi-Fi only runs while the aircraft is disarmed. Check that, or hold the board's button for 3 seconds to open it.

Remember to save

After you change anything, press Save settings at the bottom of the page.

Step 4

Pair your camera

[[shot name="pair_modal" cap="Pairing a GoPro."]]

GoPro

  1. In the configurator open Binding and press Pair GoPro.
  2. On the camera: swipe down, Preferences → Connections → Connect Device → The Remote.
  3. When the camera is pairing, press Scan now and tap your camera in the list.

DJI Osmo

  1. Press Pair DJI, then put the camera in pairing mode and accept the prompt on its screen.

Pair once. After that RecLink reconnects by itself, and picks the strongest one if you have several (up to four).

Step 5

Make it record

Choose when the camera records. There are two common ways. Pick one.

Record when you arm. Nothing to change. This is the default: the camera starts when you arm and stops when you disarm.

[[shot name="cc_record" cap="Cam control → Recording, radio switch mode." legend="1|The recording mode.;2|Switch or Button.;3|The AUX channel.;4|Detects the channel.;5|Live position of the control.;6|On above this value."]]

Record from a radio switch. You choose when to film with a switch on your transmitter.

  1. Open Cam control. Under Start / stop recording choose From a radio switch.
  2. Press Set AUX, then flip your switch within 15 seconds. RecLink picks the channel that moved and fills in the AUX channel box. The flight controller must be connected.
  3. Leave Trigger type on Switch: switch on means record, switch off means stop. For a spring-loaded button choose Button: each press starts or stops.
  4. Flip the switch and watch the bar. The amber line must cross the On above value (default 1700 µs) when the switch is on, and stay below it when off.
  5. Press Save settings. Flip the switch: the camera starts, and the Status tab shows REC.

Which switch position counts as on depends on the threshold. A 3-position switch usually works with its top position as on.

Combine arm and switch

Armed OR switch onRecords when armed or when the switch is on. While armed, switching off cuts the clip.
Armed AND switch onRecords only when both are true. Releasing either stops the clip.

Step 6

See it on your OSD

[[shot name="osd_preview" cap="OSD tab: what your goggles show."]]

RecLink writes four lines of text into the OSD elements you placed in step 2.

REC 12:34Recording, and for how long
###-78%Camera battery
4K60 WIDEResolution, frame rate and lens
SD 1HRecording time left on the card
CAM LOSTThe camera is not connected

You can change the lines on the OSD tab. Pick OSD type Digital for DJI-style goggles, Analog for an analog OSD.

The LED

LEDMeaning
2 short blinks every secondWi-Fi is on, you can configure
1 short blink every 3 secondsAll good, camera connected
SolidCamera is recording
Slow fade in and outLooking for the camera
Fast blinkingCamera link lost
2 short blinks every 2 secondsNo link to the flight controller

The button

Quick pressThe LED blinks for 3 seconds, so you can find the board
Hold 3 secondsTurns the Wi-Fi on or off
Hold 10 secondsFactory reset. Erases settings and camera pairings. Not while armed

If something is wrong

ProblemTry this
No RecLink Wi-FiDisarm the quad. Hold the button for 3 seconds.
The page will not openType http://10.0.0.1. Stay on the Wi-Fi even without internet.
Nothing on the OSDCheck Betaflight is 2025.12 or newer, MSP is on for that UART, and Custom message 1–4 are placed. Check the wires: TX to RX, RX to TX.
CAM LOSTTurn the camera on and wait. If it was never paired, do step 4.
Camera not found when pairingPut the camera in pairing mode first, then press Scan now. Disconnect it from other devices.
